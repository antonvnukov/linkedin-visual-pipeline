# Format gates: card-flow

1. The four nodes describe one ordered sequence, not four parallel categories. If the
   order could be shuffled without changing the meaning, this is the wrong format.
2. The connectors trace the reading path unambiguously: 01 and 02 across the top, down
   the right, then back left to the payoff. A reader who scans left-to-right along the
   bottom row meets 04 before 03, so the numbers and arrows have to carry that turn.
3. The accent node is the payoff, not merely the last step. It states the rule the
   preceding three steps earn.
4. Each node's kicker names something concrete (tools, inputs, owners). A kicker that
   restates the label in other words is wasted space on the card's densest surface.
5. The insight lines are claims, not captions: each says what the step buys or costs,
   not what it is.
