# Format gates: motion

1. Frame 0 is a complete, readable poster on its own; it is also the video thumbnail.
2. Frame 0 and the closing hold are pixel-identical, so the loop does not jump.
3. Movement carries meaning. No decorative wipes, zoom packs, particles, gradient sweeps,
   or spring overshoot.
4. The layout stays spatially stable; time resolves density rather than rearranging it.
5. Nothing critical is legible only in frames a scrolling viewer would miss.
6. The GIF is within LinkedIn's pixel and file-size budget at its delivered dimensions.

- In a card-flow, layers light strictly in sequence and never two at once. Simultaneous entrances contradict the gating argument the format exists to make.
