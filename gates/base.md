# Base gates

Inherited by every artifact, authored once, never retyped. These are free: they cost
no model call to produce and they never need regenerating.

1. The central claim registers within one second at feed size, without zooming.
2. Every text element is legible at 504px wide. At a 390px phone a 1080px card renders
   at roughly 0.36x, so anything under 15px source is unreadable and more than two text
   columns will not survive.
3. Typeface and palette are the brand's, applied consistently. A single typeface across
   the whole card is the brand lock, not a mistake.
4. Every figure shown is covered by the claims ledger, and none appears more prominently
   than its cap allows. The same statistic rendered twice reads as two findings.
5. The footer is complete and balanced. Identity on one side with nothing on the other
   reads as unfinished, and identity must not be duplicated between header and footer.
6. No em or en dashes anywhere in the copy.
7. No generic-AI tells: side stripes or accent rails, gradient text, identical filler grids,
   or eyebrows and labels that carry no information. A large hero figure is the intended
   design of several formats here and is NOT a defect on its own; it is only the
   hero-metric cliche when nothing supports it, meaning no headline that interprets the
   number, no source, and no structure beneath it.
8. Whitespace is deliberate. The composition is neither top-heavy nor floating in a void,
   and the eye path through the card is unambiguous.
