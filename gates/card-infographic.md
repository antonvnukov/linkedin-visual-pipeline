# Format gates: card-infographic

1. The nodes are ONE ordered list read top to bottom along the path. If the items could be
   shuffled without changing the meaning, a dashboard or a quote card says it better.
2. Every `fix` is actionable and sourced: an imperative the reader can do, quoted or tightly
   paraphrased from a named source. A fix that restates the failure name is wasted space.
3. The path reads unambiguously: 01 at the top right turn, alternating sides, 09 (the last)
   inverted. A reader following the line never meets a number out of order.
4. The scale caption says WHOSE thresholds these are. An example threshold without its owner
   reads as a rule, and that is a claim the source never made.
5. The spec strip and readout carry only figures that are in the claims ledger, and nothing in
   the image contradicts the post it ships with.
6. Density is the point, but every text line stays at or above 12.5px at 1080 wide. If it does
   not fit, cut a node, never shrink the type.
7. (cycle) The six steps are a LOOP: step 06 must lead back into step 01 in the real process.
   If it does not repeat, it is a sequence and belongs on the spine layout.
8. (cycle) A quote carries its source, and the quote is verbatim, not paraphrased.
